Loading the numbers…

Japan in Numbers is an independent, unofficial reference. Figures come from Japan’s official statistics — the Statistics Bureau (MIC), Cabinet Office, MHLW, MEXT, the Ministry of Finance, GSI, JNTO and the OECD — and each card names its source and year.

A static site: no accounts, no tracking, no network calls beyond its own files. Census counts are 2020 unless a card says otherwise; 2024 vital figures are MHLW’s final counts.
